How do I care for my flowers?

Keep your flowers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Avoid water or mist, as our flowers are handmade and not real. Gently dust with a soft brush, or a blow dryer on a cool setting, if needed.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time varies based on your location and the complexity of your order. Once details are confirmed, we'll share the estimated delivery time with you.

Where do you deliver, and how much is it?

We deliver only in Kharagpur. Pickup is free. Day delivery is ₹30 and night delivery is ₹40.
